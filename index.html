02 — OUR STORY

A small center with
a big heart.

New Hope Animalry began in 2014 in Gilbert, Arizona with one simple belief: a puppy is not a purchase, it is a promise. We keep our litters small, raise them in a home rather than a kennel, and we stay in touch with every family long after adoption day.

Shih Tzu are companion dogs by nature — bred for centuries to live close to people. That is why every puppy here is handled daily, socialised with children and other pets, and matched carefully with the right household.

03 — THE PROCESS

Three calm steps
to adoption day.

STEP 01 1

Choose your puppy

Browse the profiles above and shortlist the puppies whose personality fits your home. Not sure? Send us a message and we will help you compare.

  • ✓ Real photos, updated weekly
  • ✓ Temperament notes for every puppy
  • ✓ Transparent, negotiable fees
STEP 02 2

Meet & ask questions

We arrange a visit at our Gilbert location, or a video call if you are travelling. You will see the puppy with their mother, review health records and ask us anything.

  • ✓ In-person or video introduction
  • ✓ Full health record review
  • ✓ No pressure, no deposit to visit
STEP 03 3

Welcome them home

Complete the adoption agreement, receive the health certificate, vaccination card and microchip details — then take your new family member home.

  • ✓ Health certificate & vaccine card
  • ✓ Microchip registration
  • ✓ 6-month follow-up check-in
